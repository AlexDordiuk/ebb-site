Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

In short: Ebb has no account, no ads and no tracking. What you log stays on your devices and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud. The app can send anonymous usage statistics (which screens and features are used). They never include what you log, and you can turn them off at any time.

Ebb is an app that helps you cut down or quit nicotine pouches and snus. It is made by Oleksandr Dordiuk, an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your device

When you use Ebb, the app stores the following on your iPhone:

  • your log entries: time, product, nicotine strength and optional notes;
  • your plan: mode (cut down or quit), daily limits and start dates;
  • cravings you record: time, strength, trigger and outcome;
  • settings such as product prices, currency and notification preferences.

This data is shared only with Ebb's own widgets and, if you use one, Ebb on your Apple Watch. The phone and the watch exchange it directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity. It is never sent to us.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you have Ebb Pro and turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) under your Apple ID, so it can appear on your other devices. We cannot see or access this data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Purchases of Ebb Pro are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check that a purchase is valid and to unlock Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, information about your purchases and transactions, and basic technical data such as device model, OS version, app version and country. It does not receive your name or email. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features help and where the app needs work, Ebb can send anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service from Germany (servers in Germany and the Netherlands).

What is sent:

  • that the app was opened on a given day, and which screens and features were used, such as the widget, Apple Watch, SOS exercises, charts or dreams. Features are sent as a summary the next day, not at the moment you use them;
  • the steps of the first setup, the mode you chose (cut down or quit) and the product type (pouches, snus, or dip and chew);
  • when the Pro offer is shown and whether a purchase was started, completed or cancelled, with the plan (yearly or lifetime), but not the price or payment details;
  • what is set up: which Ebb widgets are installed, whether Ebb is on your Apple Watch, whether notifications and iCloud sync are on, the app icon and how many dreams you have (none, 1–3 or more);
  • which notification you opened and which kinds of notifications you turned on or off;
  • technical information: crash and freeze types reported by iOS, error codes, device model, iOS and app version, language, region setting and time zone.

What is never sent: your log entries, how many pouches you use, nicotine strength, prices, money saved, notes, cravings and their outcome, relapses, milestones, the names or prices of your dreams, or the times when you log.

Statistics are tied to a random identifier created by the app. It is not your Apple ID, it is not linked to your purchases, and TelemetryDeck stores it only as a salted hash. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses. We cannot tell who you are from this data.

In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, statistics are off until you turn them on during setup, and we process them only with this consent. Elsewhere they are on by default, and the setup screen says so. Everywhere you can turn them off in Settings › Anonymous analytics. This stops the statistics and deletes the identifier and anything not yet sent from your device.

We see the statistics in TelemetryDeck for up to 24 months. TelemetryDeck may keep the anonymous raw events longer in its archive.

App Store statistics

If you allowed sharing with app developers in iOS (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple shares aggregated App Store statistics and crash reports with us. Apple decides what is included; see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders and milestone notifications are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking, and the app does not ask for permission to track you.
  • We don't combine the statistics with your purchases or any other data about you.

Health information

Entries about nicotine use are personal, which is why they stay on your devices and in your own iCloud and are never part of the statistics. Ebb is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. If you used iCloud sync, you can also remove Ebb's data in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage. To stop the statistics and delete the analytics identifier, turn off Settings › Anonymous analytics in Ebb. Because the statistics are anonymous, we cannot find or delete events that were already sent for a particular person.

Age

Ebb is intended for adults (18+).
